### Short description (max 80 characters)
Log food, symptoms & environment. AI reveals your allergy triggers.

### Full description

Struggling with allergies but don't know what's causing them? AllergyTrack helps you find the answer.

Log your food, allergy symptoms, and environmental conditions — the app analyzes patterns and reveals what's actually triggering your reactions.
